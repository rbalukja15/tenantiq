SUPPORT AND SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT
Northwind Technologies Ltd — Enterprise Plan
Effective 1 February 2026

1. SUPPORT HOURS AND CHANNELS
Standard support is available 09:00 to 18:00 UK time, Monday to Friday, excluding England and Wales
public holidays. Severity 1 incidents are supported twenty-four hours a day, seven days a week,
including public holidays. Tickets are raised through the support portal or by email to the address
named in the Order Form. Telephone support is available for Severity 1 incidents only, using the
escalation number provided at onboarding.

2. SEVERITY DEFINITIONS
Severity 1 (Critical): the production service is completely unavailable, or customer data is at risk
of loss or corruption, and no workaround exists. Severity 2 (High): a major function is unavailable
or severely degraded for multiple users, and any workaround is impractical for normal operation.
Severity 3 (Medium): a function is impaired for some users, or a defect exists for which a reasonable
workaround is available. Severity 4 (Low): a cosmetic defect, a documentation error, or a general
question with no operational impact.

3. RESPONSE AND RESOLUTION TARGETS
Initial response is measured from ticket receipt to a substantive human reply, not an automated
acknowledgement. For Severity 1 the initial response target is thirty (30) minutes and the target for
a resolution or a documented workaround is four (4) hours. For Severity 2 the initial response target
is two (2) business hours and the workaround target is one (1) business day. For Severity 3 the
initial response target is one (1) business day and the resolution target is five (5) business days.
For Severity 4 the initial response target is two (2) business days and resolution is delivered in a
future release at Provider's discretion.

Provider shall meet the initial response target for at least ninety-five percent (95%) of tickets in
any calendar quarter, measured per severity level and reported in the quarterly service review.

4. UPTIME COMMITMENT AND SERVICE CREDITS
Provider commits to a monthly uptime of ninety-nine point nine percent (99.9%), measured as the
percentage of one-minute intervals in the calendar month during which the service responded
successfully to the automated availability probe. Scheduled maintenance within an announced window,
and downtime attributable to Customer's own systems or to a force majeure event, are excluded from
the calculation.

Where monthly uptime falls below the commitment, Customer may claim service credits against the
following month's invoice: below 99.9% but at or above 99.5%, a credit of ten percent (10%) of the
monthly fee; below 99.5% but at or above 99.0%, a credit of twenty-five percent (25%); below 99.0%,
a credit of fifty percent (50%). Credits are the sole and exclusive remedy for failure to meet the
uptime commitment. A claim must be submitted within thirty (30) days of the end of the affected month
and must include the dates and times of the unavailability Customer observed.

5. SCHEDULED MAINTENANCE
Routine maintenance is performed within the announced window of Sunday 02:00 to 06:00 UK time.
Provider shall give at least five (5) business days notice of maintenance expected to cause an
interruption, and at least twenty-four (24) hours notice of emergency maintenance required to address
a security vulnerability or an imminent stability risk. Provider targets no more than eight (8) hours
of interrupting maintenance in any rolling twelve (12) month period.

6. ESCALATION
If a Severity 1 incident is not resolved within four (4) hours, it is escalated automatically to the
Head of Engineering. If it is not resolved within eight (8) hours, it is escalated to the Chief
Technology Officer, who shall provide Customer with written updates at least every two (2) hours
until resolution. Customer may request escalation of any ticket through its named account manager.

7. EXCLUSIONS
The targets in this Agreement do not apply to: beta or preview features explicitly labelled as such;
issues caused by Customer's modification of the service or use outside the documentation; and issues
arising from third-party services Customer has integrated that are outside Provider's control.

8. SERVICE REVIEW
Provider and Customer shall hold a quarterly service review covering ticket volumes by severity,
attainment against response targets, uptime attainment, incidents and their post-incident reviews,
and the forward roadmap. The review is held remotely unless the parties agree otherwise.
