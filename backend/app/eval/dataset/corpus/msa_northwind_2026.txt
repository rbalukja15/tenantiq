MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
Between Northwind Technologies Ltd ("Provider") and Acme Industrial Group ("Customer")
Effective 1 February 2026

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES
Provider shall supply the hosted document intelligence platform, professional services, and support
described in each Order Form executed under this Agreement. Each Order Form incorporates these terms
by reference. Where an Order Form conflicts with this Agreement, the Order Form governs, but only for
the services described in that Order Form and only where the conflict is explicit.

2. TERM AND RENEWAL
The initial term of this Agreement is twenty-four (24) months from the Effective Date. Thereafter the
Agreement renews automatically for successive twelve (12) month periods unless either party gives
written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.
Renewal pricing shall not increase by more than five percent (5%) over the immediately preceding
term unless the parties agree otherwise in writing.

3. FEES AND PAYMENT
Fees are stated in each Order Form and are exclusive of value added tax and other applicable taxes.
Provider shall invoice Customer monthly in arrears. Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within
thirty (30) days of the invoice date. Amounts properly disputed in good faith are not due until the
dispute is resolved, provided Customer notifies Provider of the dispute in writing within fifteen (15)
days of receiving the invoice and pays the undisputed portion when due. Overdue undisputed amounts
accrue interest at one percent (1%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower.

4. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving one hundred and twenty (120) days
prior written notice. Either party may terminate for cause if the other party commits a material
breach and fails to cure that breach within thirty (30) days of written notice describing it in
reasonable detail. Provider may suspend the services on ten (10) business days notice if Customer
fails to pay undisputed amounts that are more than sixty (60) days overdue.

5. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
Except for the excluded claims described below, each party's total aggregate liability arising out of
or related to this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid or payable by Customer in the twelve
(12) months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim. Neither party shall be liable
for indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or punitive damages, or for loss of profits,
revenue, or anticipated savings, even if advised of the possibility of such damages.

The limitation in this section does not apply to: a party's indemnification obligations; Customer's
obligation to pay fees due; either party's breach of its confidentiality obligations; or liability
that cannot be limited under applicable law. Provider's liability for a breach of its data protection
obligations is capped at three (3) times the fees paid in the preceding twelve (12) months.

6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
Provider retains all right, title, and interest in the platform, including all improvements and
derivative works. Customer retains all right, title, and interest in Customer Data. Customer grants
Provider a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence to process Customer Data solely to provide
and support the services, and to produce aggregated, de-identified statistics that do not identify
Customer or any individual.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY
Each party shall protect the other's Confidential Information with at least the degree of care it
uses for its own confidential information, and in no event less than reasonable care. Confidentiality
obligations survive termination for five (5) years, except for trade secrets, which are protected for
as long as they remain trade secrets under applicable law.

8. DATA RETURN AND DELETION
On termination or expiry, Provider shall make Customer Data available for export in a machine-readable
format for thirty (30) days. After that period Provider shall delete Customer Data from production
systems within sixty (60) days and from encrypted backups within one hundred and eighty (180) days,
and shall certify deletion in writing on request.

9. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES
This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales, without regard to conflict of law rules.
The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales. Before commencing
proceedings, the parties shall escalate the dispute to their respective executive sponsors, who shall
meet within twenty (20) business days to attempt resolution in good faith.

10. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBCONTRACTING
Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's prior written consent, which shall not be
unreasonably withheld, except that either party may assign to a successor in connection with a merger
or sale of substantially all assets on written notice. Provider may engage subcontractors provided it
remains fully responsible for their performance and for their compliance with this Agreement.
