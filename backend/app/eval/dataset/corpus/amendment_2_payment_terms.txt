AMENDMENT NO. 2 TO THE MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
Between Northwind Technologies Ltd and Acme Industrial Group
Effective 1 July 2026

BACKGROUND
The parties entered into a Master Services Agreement effective 1 February 2026 (the "Agreement").
Amendment No. 1, effective 1 April 2026, adjusted the professional services rate card and is not
affected by this Amendment. The parties now wish to amend the payment provisions of the Agreement as
set out below. Capitalised terms not defined here have the meanings given in the Agreement.

1. AMENDED PAYMENT PERIOD
Section 3 (Fees and Payment) of the Agreement is amended by deleting the words "within thirty (30)
days of the invoice date" and replacing them with "within forty-five (45) days of the invoice date".
For the avoidance of doubt, the payment period under the Agreement is now forty-five (45) days from
the invoice date for all invoices issued on or after the effective date of this Amendment. Invoices
issued before that date remain payable on the previous thirty (30) day terms.

2. EARLY PAYMENT DISCOUNT
A new sub-section is added to Section 3. Where Customer pays an undisputed invoice in full within ten
(10) days of the invoice date, Customer may deduct an early payment discount of two percent (2%) of
the invoice value excluding tax. The early payment discount may not be combined with any other
discount, credit, or rebate offered under the Agreement or any Order Form, and does not apply to
pass-through expenses or third-party licence fees invoiced at cost.

3. DISPUTE WINDOW
The period for notifying a disputed invoice is extended from fifteen (15) days to twenty (20) days
from receipt of the invoice. The remainder of the dispute mechanism in Section 3 is unchanged,
including the requirement that Customer pay the undisputed portion of any disputed invoice when due.

4. LATE PAYMENT INTEREST
The interest rate on overdue undisputed amounts is reduced from one percent (1%) per month to
zero point seven five percent (0.75%) per month, or the maximum rate permitted by law if lower.
Interest accrues from the day after the applicable payment period expires.

5. SUSPENSION THRESHOLD
Provider's right to suspend the services for non-payment is amended so that suspension may be
exercised only where undisputed amounts are more than ninety (90) days overdue, rather than sixty
(60) days. The notice period before suspension remains ten (10) business days.

6. INVOICING MECHANICS
Provider shall issue invoices electronically to the billing contact named in the applicable Order
Form, in PDF format, with a machine-readable summary in CSV attached. Provider shall include the
Customer purchase order number on every invoice where Customer has supplied one. An invoice that
omits a required purchase order number is not properly issued, and the payment period does not begin
until a corrected invoice is received.

7. ANNUAL REVIEW OF PAYMENT TERMS
The parties shall review the payment terms in this Amendment at each anniversary of the Effective
Date of the Agreement. Neither party is obliged to agree to a change, and the terms in this Amendment
continue to apply unless and until replaced by a further written amendment signed by both parties.

8. NO OTHER CHANGES
Except as expressly amended by this Amendment, all terms of the Agreement, including the limitation
of liability in Section 5, the termination provisions in Section 4, and the data return and deletion
obligations in Section 8, remain in full force and effect. In the event of a conflict between this
Amendment and the Agreement, this Amendment governs with respect to payment terms only.

9. COUNTERPARTS
This Amendment may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original and all of which
together constitute one instrument. Electronic signatures are valid and binding.
