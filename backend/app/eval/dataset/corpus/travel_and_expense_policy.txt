TRAVEL AND EXPENSE POLICY
Northwind Technologies Ltd — Internal, Version 7
Effective 1 January 2026

1. PRINCIPLES
Spend the company's money as carefully as you would spend your own, and no more carefully than you
would spend your own. Book what lets you do good work and arrive able to do it. If a choice is
defensible in a sentence to your manager, it is within policy. Where this policy and common sense
disagree, raise it rather than quietly picking one.

2. APPROVAL
Travel within the United Kingdom does not require prior approval if the total expected cost is under
five hundred pounds (GBP 500). International travel, and any trip expected to exceed GBP 500,
requires written approval from your manager before booking. Approval for customer-site visits should
also be recorded on the relevant account plan.

3. AIR TRAVEL
Economy class is the standard for all flights under six (6) hours of scheduled flying time. Premium
economy may be booked for flights of six (6) hours or more. Business class requires director-level
approval and is normally reserved for flights over nine (9) hours where the traveller is working on
the day of arrival. Book at least fourteen (14) days in advance where the trip is known that far
ahead; late bookings need a one-line justification in the expense claim.

4. ACCOMMODATION
The maximum reimbursable rate for a hotel night is two hundred pounds (GBP 200) in London,
one hundred and forty pounds (GBP 140) elsewhere in the United Kingdom, and the local equivalent of
one hundred and eighty pounds (GBP 180) for international travel. These caps include local taxes and
exclude breakfast where it is charged separately. If no room is available within the cap, book the
cheapest suitable room and note the reason on the claim.

5. MEALS AND SUBSISTENCE
Reasonable meal costs are reimbursed on production of a receipt, up to forty pounds (GBP 40) per day
in the United Kingdom and fifty-five pounds (GBP 55) per day internationally. Alcohol is not
reimbursable except as part of a customer or team meal that has been approved in advance. There is no
per diem; claim what you actually spent.

6. GROUND TRANSPORT
Standard class rail is the default for domestic journeys. First class may be booked where it is
cheaper than standard on the day, or for journeys over three (3) hours where the traveller intends to
work. Taxis and ride-hailing are reimbursable for journeys where public transport is impractical,
unsafe, or would take more than forty-five (45) minutes longer. Mileage in a private vehicle is
reimbursed at the HMRC approved rate.

7. REMOTE AND HOME WORKING
Employees working from home may claim a one-off home office allowance of three hundred pounds
(GBP 300) in their first year, and a further one hundred and fifty pounds (GBP 150) in any subsequent
year, for equipment that supports safe and comfortable working. Monitors, chairs, desks, and
peripherals qualify. Consumables, furniture unrelated to work, and general household costs do not.

8. SUBMITTING CLAIMS
Submit claims within thirty (30) days of the expense being incurred, with itemised receipts.
Claims older than ninety (90) days are reimbursed only with finance director approval. Reimbursement
is paid with the next payroll run after approval. Claims submitted without receipts are reimbursed
only where the amount is under twenty-five pounds (GBP 25) and the purpose is described.

9. CORPORATE CARDS
Corporate cards are issued to employees who travel more than four (4) times a year. Personal spend on
a corporate card must be repaid within seven (7) days of the statement date. Repeated personal use
may result in the card being withdrawn.

10. EXCEPTIONS
Exceptions to this policy are granted by the finance director and are recorded with the reason. A
pattern of exceptions in the same area is treated as a signal that the policy needs changing, and is
reviewed at the annual policy review.
