ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY
Northwind Technologies Ltd — Platform Terms
Effective 1 February 2026

1. PURPOSE
This policy sets out what customers and their authorised users may and may not do with the Northwind
platform. It supplements the Master Services Agreement and applies to every account, environment, and
API credential issued to a customer. Breach of this policy may lead to suspension under the
suspension provisions of the Agreement.

2. AUTHORISED USERS
Access credentials are issued to named individuals and must not be shared. Customer is responsible for
the acts and omissions of its authorised users as if they were Customer's own. Customer shall revoke
access promptly when an authorised user leaves or changes role, and in any event within five (5)
business days. Service accounts used for API integrations must be clearly labelled, owned by a named
individual, and reviewed at least every six (6) months.

3. PROHIBITED CONTENT
Customer shall not upload content that it does not have the right to process, that infringes a third
party's intellectual property, or that contains malicious code. Customer shall not upload special
categories of personal data, payment card data subject to PCI DSS, or classified government material,
unless the parties have agreed additional controls in writing and recorded them in an Order Form.

4. PROHIBITED CONDUCT
Customer shall not: attempt to gain unauthorised access to any part of the platform or to another
tenant's data; probe, scan, or test the vulnerability of the platform without Provider's prior written
consent; interfere with or disrupt the integrity or performance of the platform; reverse engineer or
attempt to derive the source code of the platform except to the extent that restriction is prohibited
by law; or use the platform to build a competing service.

5. RATE LIMITS AND FAIR USE
API usage is subject to per-tenant rate limits published in the developer documentation. The default
limits are one hundred and twenty (120) read requests per minute, twenty (20) document uploads per
minute, and thirty (30) question requests per minute. Sustained usage beyond a plan's fair use
allowance may be throttled after notice. Customer shall not attempt to circumvent rate limits by
distributing requests across multiple accounts or credentials.

6. AUTOMATED AND HIGH-VOLUME USE
Bulk ingestion of more than ten thousand (10,000) documents in any twenty-four (24) hour period
requires prior notice to Provider so that capacity can be arranged. Provider will not unreasonably
withhold agreement, and may propose a scheduled window. Automated question generation for the purpose
of benchmarking or model training is not permitted without written agreement.

7. SECURITY RESEARCH
Provider welcomes good-faith security research conducted under its published responsible disclosure
policy. Researchers must not access, modify, or exfiltrate data belonging to any party other than
themselves, must not degrade the service, and must report findings to the security contact before any
public disclosure. Provider will not pursue action against research that follows that policy.

8. REPORTING MISUSE
Suspected misuse should be reported to the abuse contact published in the developer documentation.
Provider will acknowledge a report within one (1) business day and will keep the reporter informed of
progress where it is lawful and appropriate to do so.

9. ENFORCEMENT
Provider will normally give notice and an opportunity to remedy before taking action. Where conduct
presents an immediate risk to the platform, to other customers, or to data, Provider may suspend the
affected credentials or environment immediately and give notice as soon as practicable afterwards.
Suspension under this policy does not relieve Customer of its obligation to pay fees for the period of
suspension, unless the suspension is later found to have been made in error.

10. CHANGES
Provider may update this policy on thirty (30) days notice. Where a change materially reduces
Customer's rights, Customer may terminate the affected services without penalty by giving notice
before the change takes effect.
