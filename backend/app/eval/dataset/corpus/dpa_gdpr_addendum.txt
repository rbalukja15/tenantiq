DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM
Forming part of the Master Services Agreement between Northwind Technologies Ltd ("Processor") and
Acme Industrial Group ("Controller")
Effective 1 February 2026

1. ROLES AND SCOPE
Controller determines the purposes and means of processing Personal Data. Processor processes
Personal Data only on documented instructions from Controller, including with regard to transfers to
a third country, unless required to do so by law to which Processor is subject. Processor shall
inform Controller of any such legal requirement before processing, unless that law prohibits it on
important grounds of public interest.

2. NATURE AND PURPOSE OF PROCESSING
Processor processes Personal Data contained in documents Controller uploads to the platform, for the
purpose of extraction, indexing, retrieval, and generating answers grounded in those documents. The
categories of data subject may include Controller's employees, contractors, customers, and
counterparties. Special categories of personal data should not be uploaded; where they are, Controller
warrants it has a lawful basis under Article 9 of the UK GDPR.

3. PERSONAL DATA BREACH NOTIFICATION
Processor shall notify Controller without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours,
after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach affecting Controller's Personal Data. The notification
shall describe the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of data subjects and
records concerned, the likely consequences, and the measures taken or proposed to address it. Where
the information cannot be provided at the same time, it may be provided in phases without further
undue delay. Processor shall not notify any supervisory authority or data subject on Controller's
behalf without Controller's prior written instruction.

4. SUB-PROCESSORS
Controller grants general written authorisation for Processor to engage sub-processors. The approved
sub-processors as at the Effective Date are: Amazon Web Services EMEA SARL (hosting, Ireland),
Cloudflare Inc (content delivery and denial-of-service protection, global edge), and Twilio Ireland
Limited (transactional email and notifications, Ireland). Processor shall give Controller at least
thirty (30) days prior written notice of any intended addition or replacement of a sub-processor, and
Controller may object on reasonable data protection grounds within that period.

Where Controller objects, the parties shall discuss in good faith. If no resolution is reached within
thirty (30) days of the objection, Controller may terminate the affected services without penalty on
written notice, and Processor shall refund prepaid fees for the terminated services covering the
period after termination.

5. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS
Personal Data is stored and processed in the Republic of Ireland and the United Kingdom. Processor
shall not transfer Personal Data outside the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area except
under the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, or another
transfer mechanism recognised as providing an adequate level of protection. Processor shall carry out
and document a transfer risk assessment before any such transfer and make it available on request.

6. SECURITY OF PROCESSING
Processor shall implement appropriate technical and organisational measures under Article 32 of the
UK GDPR, described in the Information Security Policy referenced in the Agreement. Processor shall
ensure that persons authorised to process Personal Data are bound by confidentiality obligations and
receive data protection training at least annually.

7. ASSISTANCE TO CONTROLLER
Taking into account the nature of the processing, Processor shall assist Controller by appropriate
technical and organisational measures in fulfilling Controller's obligation to respond to data
subject requests. Processor shall forward any request it receives directly from a data subject to
Controller within five (5) business days and shall not respond to it itself, except to confirm receipt
and to direct the data subject to Controller.

8. AUDIT AND INSPECTION
Processor shall make available all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this Addendum
and shall allow for and contribute to audits, including inspections, conducted by Controller or an
auditor mandated by Controller. Audits are limited to once in any twelve (12) month period unless a
Personal Data Breach has occurred, and shall be conducted on at least thirty (30) days notice during
normal business hours in a manner that does not unreasonably disrupt Processor's operations.

9. DELETION AND RETURN
On termination of the services, Processor shall delete or return all Personal Data at Controller's
election, and delete existing copies unless law requires storage. Deletion timescales follow the data
return and deletion provisions of the Agreement.
