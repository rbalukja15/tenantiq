INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY
Northwind Technologies Ltd — Public Summary, Version 4.2
Last reviewed: 12 January 2026

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This policy describes the technical and organisational controls Northwind applies to the hosted
platform and to the corporate systems that support it. It applies to all employees, contractors, and
subcontractors with access to production systems or customer data. It is reviewed at least annually
and after any material change to the platform architecture.

2. ENCRYPTION
All customer data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 with keys managed in a hardware security module
and rotated at least every twelve (12) months. All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3;
TLS 1.2 is permitted only for legacy integrations explicitly approved by the security team, and
TLS 1.1 and below are disabled at the load balancer. Database backups are encrypted with a separate
key hierarchy from production data, so compromise of a production key does not expose backups.

3. ACCESS CONTROL
Access to production systems requires multi-factor authentication and is granted on a least-privilege
basis through named individual accounts. Shared accounts are prohibited. Production access is granted
just-in-time through an approval workflow, expires automatically after eight (8) hours, and is logged
with the business justification recorded at the time of request. Access rights are reviewed quarterly
and revoked within twenty-four (24) hours of a leaver's last working day.

4. SECURITY INCIDENT RESPONSE
The security team operates an on-call rota with a fifteen (15) minute acknowledgement target for
alerts classified as critical. A suspected security incident is triaged within one (1) hour of
detection. Where an incident is confirmed to involve customer data, the data protection team is
engaged immediately and customer notification follows the timescales in the applicable Data
Processing Addendum. Post-incident reviews are completed within ten (10) business days and are
blameless; corrective actions are tracked to closure with named owners.

5. VULNERABILITY MANAGEMENT
Automated dependency scanning runs on every pull request and nightly against all deployed images.
Critical vulnerabilities are remediated within seven (7) days of a fix being available, high severity
within thirty (30) days, and medium within ninety (90) days. Where a fix is not available, a
documented compensating control is put in place and reviewed monthly. An external penetration test is
commissioned at least annually and the executive summary is available to customers under NDA.

6. NETWORK AND PLATFORM SECURITY
Production runs in private subnets with no direct inbound access from the internet except through the
managed load balancer and a bastion protected by MFA and hardware keys. Security groups default to
deny. Container images are built from minimal base images, scanned before promotion, and signed;
unsigned images are rejected at deploy time by admission control.

7. LOGGING AND MONITORING
Application, infrastructure, and access logs are centralised, retained for three hundred and
ninety-five (395) days, and protected against modification. Alerting covers authentication anomalies,
privilege escalation, unusual data egress volume, and configuration drift. Logs containing personal
data are subject to the same retention and deletion controls as customer data.

8. TENANT ISOLATION
The platform is multi-tenant. Every tenant-owned table carries a tenant identifier, and isolation is
enforced in two independent layers: an application-level scoped data access layer, and database
row-level security policies that apply even to the table owner. The application connects as a
non-superuser role without the ability to bypass row-level security. Isolation is verified by an
automated adversarial test suite on every change.

9. PERSONNEL SECURITY
All staff complete background screening appropriate to their role before being granted production
access, and complete security and data protection training on joining and at least annually
thereafter. Phishing simulations run quarterly. Staff who fail two consecutive simulations receive
additional targeted training.

10. BUSINESS CONTINUITY
Production data is backed up continuously with point-in-time recovery for thirty-five (35) days. The
recovery point objective is fifteen (15) minutes and the recovery time objective is four (4) hours.
Restore procedures are exercised at least twice a year and the results are recorded.
